## The Day 8 Conclusion

To wrap up this mental model:
Imagine a kitchen with 2 chefs (CPUs).

Scenario A: 2 chefs, 2 orders. Fast.

Scenario B: 2 chefs, 50 orders, but only 2 chefs can stand at the stove at once.

Scenario C: 2 chefs, but you hire 50 more "assistant chefs" to stand in the tiny kitchen.

In Scenario C, the original 2 chefs can't even move their arms to flip a pancake because the kitchen is too crowded with assistants who have nothing to do.
